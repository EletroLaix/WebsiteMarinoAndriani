## Remaining entries

- The collection names a source for entries 36, 39–41, 46, 49–50, 53–63, 65–68, 70–71, 73–76 and 78–90. Check the named edition, chapter, episode or scene before treating the note as a verbatim quote. Entries 53, 68 and 71 in particular may need both an immediate source and an earlier attribution.
- Entry 45 is recognizable as a line from Porco Rosso. Entries 4, 9, 17, 26 and 28 resemble widely circulated sayings. Their immediate source and wording still need checking.
- Entries 1–3, 5, 7–8, 14–16, 18–20, 22, 24–25, 30–34, 37, 42–44, 48 and 64 have no explicit attribution in the notes. Entry 77 includes a shortened link with no named author.
